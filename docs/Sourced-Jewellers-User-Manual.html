01
Introduction

Welcome & Overview

Sourced Jewellers CRM is the all-in-one system that runs your shop — it replaces separate spreadsheets, till systems and notebooks with one connected platform.

Everything in the business is linked together. When you ring up a sale, the system automatically reduces your stock, records the customer's purchase history, updates the day's takings, calculates staff commission, and — if the item was on consignment — flags that the supplier now needs paying. You enter information once and it flows everywhere it's needed.

What the system handles

Sell & Serve
  • · Point of Sale checkout
  • · Deposit / layaway orders
  • · Trade-ins & part-exchange
  • · Printed & emailed receipts
  • · Customer profiles & VIP tiers
Stock & Source
  • · Product inventory
  • · Barcode scanning
  • · Suppliers & spend tracking
  • · Consignment management
  • · Documents & certificates
Run & Report
  • · Profit & Loss reporting
  • · Commission & payroll
  • · Expenses & cash drawer
  • · Analytics & AI assistant
  • · Full audit trail
The golden thread A single sale touches stock, the customer record, the day's figures, commission, and consignment settlements — all automatically. That's the core idea behind the whole system: record it once, and it's everywhere.

Works on any device

The system runs in any web browser on a computer, tablet or phone. It can also be installed as an app on a device's home screen for quick access (see §20). The layout adapts to the screen size — the side menu collapses to icons on smaller screens.

Products & Inventory

The Products area is your complete stock catalogue. Each product carries its price, cost, supplier, photos, documents and live stock count — and the system tracks profit and ageing automatically.

Viewing & managing stock

Switch between List view (table) and Grid view (cards). Use the search bar (name, SKU or barcode), the sort menu (newest, name, price, margin) and the Filters panel (category, metal, karat, gemstone, supplier, location, price/margin range, age, reservation status). Quick-filter chips below the search bar narrow results in one tap.

Click View Details on any product to see stock, pricing, profit and markup, ageing status, reservation info, trade-in origin, photos, documents and consignment terms. From there you can Edit, Duplicate (copy a similar item), Adjust Stock, or jump to Sell.

Adjusting stock

Inside a product, click Adjust Stock and choose Add (delivery arrived), Remove (damage/loss) or Set exact amount (after a physical count). Enter the quantity and an optional note — a live preview shows current → adjustment → new. Always add a note so the reason is on record.

Barcode scanning

On screens with a Scan button, allow camera access and point it at the barcode — the system reads it automatically (or type it manually). Scanning is the fastest, most accurate way to find items at the till.

Status badges you'll see

In Stock Low Stock Out of Stock Reserved Consignment Trade-In / PX Aged Stock

Good to know Barcodes must be unique — the system won't let you reuse one. You can't delete a product that has been sold or has active reservations/consignments; that protects your history.

Sourcing

Suppliers

A supplier is anyone you source stock from. The system tracks every product, purchase and consignment per supplier, and shows your total spend over time.

Two types of supplier

  • Registered Supplier — a business (wholesaler, distributor, jeweller) with full contact details. Appears in dropdowns while marked Active.
  • Individual Supplier — a walk-in person selling or trading in an item; name-only, no ongoing account.

Adding a supplier

Click Add Supplier and complete the tabbed form: Details (type, name, contact person), Contact (email, phone, address) and Extras (Active toggle, tags, notes). Tags such as Wholesale Local Premium help you organise and filter suppliers.

The supplier profile

Click View Detail to open a full profile with tabs:

TabWhat it shows
OverviewContact details, spend trend, recent activity, consignment summary.
InventoryEvery product from this supplier (filter In Stock / Sold / All).
FinancialsTotal spend this year (purchases + expenses) with a monthly chart.
PX HistoryFor individuals — items this person has traded in and the allowances given.
ConsignmentsActive stock, sold-but-unsettled items, and settled payouts.
DocumentsContracts and account agreements attached to the supplier.

The Suppliers list itself can be searched, sorted (name, recently added, highest spend, most products) and filtered by type, status and tags, in card or table view.

Sell on Behalf

Consignments

A consignment item is stock you hold but don't own. You sell it, keep your agreed margin, and pay the supplier (the consignor) only once it sells. The system tracks the whole lifecycle — from intake to settlement.

1
Take in
Add the product, mark it Consignment, set the consignor & terms
2
Hold & display
Shows under "Active Stock". You owe nothing yet
3
It sells
Moves to "Sold & Unsettled" — payout now due
4
Pay out
Record Payout to the consignor
5
Settled
Logged in "Settled" with a reference

Setting up a consignment item

When adding the product, switch on Consignment Item, choose the consignment supplier, set the start and end dates, enter the terms (e.g. "50/50 split" or "supplier gets £x"), and upload the signed agreement.

The Consignments page

Summary cards show Active Stock, Unsettled Sales, Pending Payouts (£ owed), Total Consignment Value and Total Settled. Three tabs organise the work:

TabContainsAction
Active StockItems held now, with status (Active, Expiring Soon, Out of Stock).View · Export
Sold & UnsettledSold items awaiting payout; flagged Overdue after 30 days.Record Payout
SettledCompleted payouts with date and reference.View · Export

Recording a payout

On an unsettled sale, click Record Payout. The dialog shows the sale price, your margin and the amount owed. Confirm or adjust the payout amount, choose the payment date, add a settlement reference (e.g. bank transfer ID) and click Record Payment. The item moves to Settled.

Why it matters The Reports area warns you of any unsettled consignments so you never lose track of money owed to a consignor. Settle promptly to keep relationships and records clean.

Relationships

Customers (CRM)

Build a profile for every customer. The system tracks their spend, purchase history, sizes and preferences, and automatically promotes them through VIP tiers — so you can give personal, informed service.

Adding a customer

Click Add Customer and complete three tabs: Basic Info (name required; email, phone, address, notes), Special Dates (birthday, anniversary — these trigger dashboard reminders), and Preferences (ring/bracelet/necklace sizes, metal and style preferences). You're taken straight to their profile.

The customer profile

Each profile shows four headline stats — Lifetime Spend, Total Purchases, Average Order Value and Member Since — plus a progress bar toward the next VIP tier, and three tabs: Overview (contact & dates & quick notes), Purchases (full transaction history) and Preferences.

Point of Sale (Selling)

The POS is where you complete a sale. It has three panels — Product Search (left), Shopping Cart (middle) and Checkout (right) — and two modes at the top: Complete Sale and Deposit Order.

The standard sale flow

1
Add items
Search or scan, then click + to add to the cart
2
Adjust cart
Change quantity, edit price, or add a trade-in
3
Customer
Enter or look up the customer (or "Walk-in")
4
Payment
Discount if any, then choose payment method
5
Complete
Click Complete Sale → print or email the receipt

Adding & editing items

Search by name, SKU or internal SKU and click +. The cart shows stock status (Available, Reserved, Last one, Out of stock). In the cart you can change quantity with +/−, edit the unit price with the pencil (green = lower than catalogue, red = higher), remove a line with the bin, or click the name for details.

Discounts

In the Checkout panel, set a discount as a percentage (%) or a fixed amount (£). Discounts are spread across the items and applied before tax. Trade-in allowances are never reduced by a discount.

Cart totals explained

LineMeaning
SubtotalAll items × quantity at their unit prices.
DiscountAmount taken off (see above).
TaxCalculated on (subtotal − discount).
Net TotalSubtotal − discount + tax − trade-in allowances = what the customer pays.

Customer, payment & staff

Enter a customer name (look up an existing customer to auto-fill, or just type "Walk-in"). Choose a payment method — Cash, Card, Bank Transfer or Other; cash is recorded in the Cash Drawer automatically. The sale is recorded against you (Processed By) for commission and reporting.

Shop location

Once you add an item, the sale locks to that item's location — you can't mix products from two locations in one sale. To sell from another location, finish the current sale first.

Optional: signature

For high-value sales or store policy, click Capture Signature, have the customer sign on screen, and Accept. The signature is stored with the receipt.

Owner approval If a trade-in allowance is larger than the sale (a negative balance — you pay the customer), Staff cannot complete it; the screen shows "Requires Owner Approval". A Manager or Owner must approve.

After the sale — and reviewing it

A confirmation appears with the sale number and options to Print Receipt, Email Receipt, Download PDF or start a New Sale (the location is kept for convenience). You can revisit your own sales any time under My Sales; managers see all sales under Transactions.

Editing & voiding (Manager+)

From Transactions, managers can Edit a sale or Void it (with a reason). Voiding reverses the revenue, returns the stock and reverses commission — the sale is kept on record, marked VOID, never deleted.

Part Exchange

Trade-Ins / Part Exchange

A trade-in (part exchange) is when a customer hands over an old item for credit against a purchase. The system records the item, deducts the allowance from the sale, and then lets you process it into saleable stock.

Taking a trade-in at the till

In the cart, click Add Trade-In and record: item name (required), category, description, serial number, the allowance (the credit given — required), the customer's details, notes and photos. It appears in a blue "Trade-Ins" section and is subtracted from the sale total. Example: £1,200 sale − £500 trade-in = £700 to pay.

Processing trade-ins into stock

Traded-in items wait in the PX Intake queue (Products → PX Intake, Manager+). From there:

1
Review the queue
See all pending trade-ins with value and age.
2
Decide
Convert to inventory, Edit the details, or place On Hold (needs servicing, authentication, cleaning, valuation, etc.).
3
Convert to product
Set the selling price, category, SKU and condition notes, optionally link a supplier, then Create Product.
4
Now sellable
The item joins your inventory, ready to sell — carrying its trade-in origin.
Best practice Always photograph trade-ins and record serial numbers on watches and high-value pieces. Check with a manager before offering a large allowance.

14
Rewarding Sales

Commission

When commission tracking is switched on, the system calculates what each staff member earns on their sales, tracks what's been paid, and lets the Owner record payments.

How it's calculated

Commission is a percentage of either revenue (the sale total) or profit (sale minus cost), at a rate set by the Owner. Individual staff can have their own override rate.

BasisExampleResult
Revenue @ 5%£1,000 of sales£50 commission
Profit @ 10%£500 sale, £300 cost → £200 profit£20 commission

Staff view — My Commission

Each person sees their own monthly breakdown — sales count, revenue, commission owed, and how much is Paid / Partial / Unpaid. "View Sales" opens the underlying sales for that month.

Owner view — paying commission

Under Reports → Commission, expand a month to see each staff member's figures. Click Pay Commission to record a payment (amount, method, notes), or Pay All for the whole month. Set the default rate and basis, and per-staff overrides, via the Settings (gear) icon. A History view lists every past payment, and the data can be exported to CSV.

15
Outgoings

Expenses

Record every business cost — rent, utilities, wages, marketing, repairs and stock purchases — attach receipts, and let recurring costs create themselves automatically.

Recording an expense

Click Record Expense and enter the description, amount, date, category, optional supplier, payment method and notes. If VAT-registered, tick Include VAT and pick the rate (0/5/20%) — the system splits out the tax automatically (or enter a manual VAT amount). Tick Mark as COGS if the cost is directly part of stock you sell (this affects profit). Drag in up to five receipt files, then Save.

Recurring expenses

For regular bills, tick Make Recurring and choose the frequency (weekly, monthly, quarterly, annually) and next due date. The system then creates each copy automatically. Recurring rows show a Recurring badge with pause, resume, edit and stop controls.

Finding & exporting

Filter by date range, category, supplier, staff member, payment method, amount range, or COGS/recurring only. Summary figures show this month, this year, total records, monthly average and year-on-year change. Export any period to CSV or PDF, and review the built-in category and monthly-trend charts.

COGS vs operating cost COGS (cost of goods sold) reduces gross profit; operating expenses (rent, marketing, wages) reduce net profit. Marking expenses correctly keeps your P&L accurate.

Deeper Insight

Analytics, AI & Audit

Beyond reports, the system offers deeper analytics, a built-in AI assistant you can simply ask questions, and a complete audit trail of every change.

Enhanced Analytics (Owner)

Tabs cover an Overview (product performance, supplier analysis, system activity, stock health), Product Mix (profitability by product), Suppliers (in-depth spend analysis), Audit Trail, and Alerts (out-of-stock and low-stock items).

The AI assistant

Click the chat bubble in the bottom-right corner and ask plain-English questions about your business — it pulls live data and can show mini-charts. For example:

  • "How much did I spend on marketing this month?"
  • "Which product is our best seller?"
  • "What's my profit margin this year?"
  • "How many items are out of stock?"

Be specific for the best answers, and ask follow-up questions in the same conversation.

Audit trail

Every create, edit and delete is logged with who, when, what changed, and the old & new values. Filter by area, action, person or date, toggle financial changes only to spot price edits, click any row for the full before/after, and export to CSV. This is your safeguard for accountability and accuracy.

Till Reconciliation

Cash Drawer

The Cash Drawer tracks physical cash per location, so you can reconcile the till at the end of the day. Cash sales feed in automatically.

Daily use

  • Set Float (Owner) — set the expected opening balance at the start of the day, e.g. £200.
  • Add — record cash put in (petty-cash top-up, cash back from the bank).
  • Remove — record cash taken out; a reason is required (bank deposit, petty-cash spend).
  • History — a full timeline of every movement with amounts and notes, for reconciliation.

Each shop location has its own drawer card showing the current expected balance and when it was last updated.

Install as an app (PWA)

Under Settings → Data → Install App, click Install to add Sourced CRM to a device's home screen or desktop. It then launches in its own window, loads faster and works like a native app — ideal for a shop tablet.

Working offline

If the internet drops, an indicator appears bottom-right: previously loaded data stays viewable, but checkout and live syncing are disabled. When the connection returns, the app reconnects and syncs automatically.

During an outage You can still look up products, customers and past sales, but you cannot complete a sale until you're back online. For a planned outage, take manual notes and enter the sales once reconnected.
